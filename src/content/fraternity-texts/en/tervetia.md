Tervetia, a student fraternity of the University of Latvia, was founded in Riga on 30 April 1922. Until 1926 its founders and later members were drawn solely from active and retired officers of the Latvian army who were studying at the University of Latvia. After 1926 Tervetia began to admit students of the University who were not officers as well.

The motto of Tervetia is “Visu par Latviju – Visi viens” (All for Latvia – all as one), and its colours are orange, silver and blue.

The idea of the colours: after a reddened morning the silver sun rises in the blue sky of the fatherland. The name Tervetia is derived from Tērvete, the name of the historic castle whose brave men, the sons of Zemgale, fought heroically against the invaders.

The convent’s coat of arms shows plainly the qualities and leanings of the spirit that Tervetia holds dear. The rays of the sun that has risen in the new age lift up in honour the helmet of the ancient Latvians, and these gifts receive their polish by plunging into the deep pools of learning.

The colour cap of Tervetia is the first on which the ornament of the star is matched to the Latvian style.

Tervetia was admitted to the Presidium Convent on 17 March 1925. The Philisters’ Society of Tervetia was founded in 1927. In the time of Latvia’s independence it already had about 100 members.

Tervetia concluded a treaty of friendship with the fraternity Fraternitas Academica on 23 November 1934, and a cartel of friendship with Ugala, the Estonian student fraternity of the University of Tartu, on 15 December 1934.

The founders of Tervetia vouched for the A!K!K! with Fraternitas Lettica. In the Year of Terror and the Second World War the family of Tervetia suffered heavy losses in its ranks. By the 2nd semester of 1988, 372 members had sworn loyalty to the flag of Tervetia. In exile 127 new members were admitted. The fraternity has 116 members, scattered across the world in seven groups: in the USA, Canada, Sweden and Australia, with individual members in Germany and Latvia. Most live in the USA (76). The members keep in touch through regular circulars. Every five years the album Tervetia is published. Tervetia presided over P!K! in 1928 and over L!K!A! in 1966 and 1985. On 29 June 1989 the convent of Tervetia resumed its work at the Latvian State University.
