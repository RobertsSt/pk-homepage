## About Vendia

![Members of Vendia in coats and green caps on steps with the fraternity’s flag; in front, three members wearing colour sashes](@/assets/fraternities/vendia/photo-1.jpg)

Founded – 20 March 1927\
Guarantor of the comment – Selonija\
Active chapters abroad – Canada\
Active members – 54

## Pastimes

![About fifteen members of Vendia in shirts and green caps, glasses in hand, on the terrace of a brick building](@/assets/fraternities/vendia/photo-2.jpg)

The family of Vendia has members with many different interests. Some take an active part in quiz games, competing for the best results both as one team and in different teams. Others are active in sport and win medals at the Latvian championships, in traditional and in extreme sports alike. Members of Vendia are also well represented in team sports competitions. Every year they hold an internal bowling tournament, and in spring most of them take the physical fitness tests of the National Guard.

## History

![A historical sheet headed “1927. g. 20. martā. Vendias dibinātāji” (20 March 1927. The founders of Vendia), with portraits of the founders in ovals](@/assets/fraternities/vendia/photo-3.jpg)

The founders of Vendia, 20 March 1927

After Latvia’s War of Independence, life in the free state flourished rapidly. A large part of Latvia’s young people sought a higher education at the new University of Latvia. Many students joined the Latvian student fraternities founded earlier, but many others could not get into them, because the number admitted was limited. So new fraternities were founded, and one of them was Vendia.

The idea of founding a new fraternity came in 1926 to eight students of the University of Latvia, who often met and talked over the possibilities. At last, when the idea had ripened and new participants had been found, these eight initiators signed the founding minutes of a new fraternity on 20 March 1927, in the flat of the agriculture student H. Zīle at Artilērijas iela 15 in Riga. They chose the name Vendia, derived from Wenden, the old name of Cēsis, which in turn comes from the name of the long-vanished tribe of the Vends. The initiators chose the colours violet, red and silver. They decided to vouch for the A!K!K! with the fraternity Selonija.

The new fraternity was registered at the Riga Regional Court and notified to the rector of the University. Soon many older students joined the initiators as founders, and their number reached 44. When an older fraternity raised objections to the colours chosen, Vendia, with the goodwill of Selonija and its permission to use its green, adopted the colours green, brown and gold. Symbolically: green speaks of hope and duty, brown greets the love of the fatherland, and may our honour shine like gold.

The aim of Vendia was, and still is, to lead its members, who are gaining academic knowledge at the universities, into public work, to cultivate and shape in them love of the fatherland and a national bearing, and to bring them up in the spirit and traditions of the fraternities.

In its first years the work of Vendia consisted in shaping its internal life, drawing up its comments and forming friendly relations with the existing fraternities. On 9 December 1930 Vendia was admitted to P!K!. In 1928 a Philisters’ Society was founded, which was admitted to the F!B!S! in 1931. In 1938 the Philisters’ Society of Vendia had 86 philisters.

Among the founders of Vendia were many who had fought in the War of Independence, and later many senior and junior officers of the Latvian army joined its family. Its members included 8 knights of the military Order of Lāčplēsis. Vendia had formed close ties of friendship with the Separate Artillery Division in Cēsis.

In the autumn semester of 1940 it was Vendia’s turn to preside over P!K!, but the communists, invading Latvia, put an end to the active work of Vendia and of all the other fraternities. The losses of Vendia during the occupations by foreign powers are very great: 21 deported, 5 murdered, 7 fallen in the war, and very many missing, so that nothing is known of their fate.

In exile Vendia resumed its work in 1950 in the USA, where most of its members were. The restored flag of Vendia, given by the then senior, J. Zaķis, was consecrated at the commemoration of 30 years since the founding, in New York, and the restored coat of arms at the celebration of 40 years, in Hamilton, Canada. By 1988, 307 members had sworn loyalty to the colours of Vendia.

When in 1956 the convent of L!K!A! decided to return to presiding in rotation, following the practice of P!K!, Vendia was, by decision of L!K!A!, the first to take on the presiding of L!K!A!, in 1957. Vendia presided over L!K!A! a second time in the academic year 1974/75.

In loyalty and love of the fatherland the members of Vendia work, individually and in public life, for the recovery of Latvia’s freedom, believing in the victory of justice in the future.
