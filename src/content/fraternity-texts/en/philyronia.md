The coat of arms of Philyronia bears the arms of the town of Liepāja, a lime tree with a lion, and as it was founded in Liepāja, its first commers was celebrated in Durbe, which is close to Liepāja.

Philyronia vouched for the A!K!K! with Lettonia, with whose members living in Liepāja the founders of Philyronia worked together in friendship.

P!K! admitted Philyronia on 11 May 1926. So 11 May is a date of more than one meaning for Philyronia.

In 1925 the Philisters’ Aid Society of Philyronia was founded in Liepāja; it too joined the Union of Philisters’ Societies.

In its Riga period Philyronia admitted 17 honorary philisters. In the autumn of 1939 it had more than 200 members. The number of members lost in the years of occupation and war is great. Early in 1941 the Cheka surprised, arrested and destroyed 22 members of the younger semesters who had gathered for a secret meeting of resistance. When the work of Philyronia was restored in the free world in 1950, only 56 members had been traced. Later the number traced grew, but not all of them remained active members of the convent. Outside Latvia 34 students have been admitted to Philyronia. The largest number of active members has been 75.

The work of Philyronia outside Latvia is coordinated by its Internal Comment. In exile 6 chapters were formed. The largest of them, in Brisbane, Australia, built convent quarters with the work and means of its own members, and with financial support from the Global Convent, on a plot of land given by the father of commilito Juris Kažoks, which encouraged the chapter’s lively activity.

Philyronia presided over L!K!A! in 1968/69 and celebrated its 50th anniversary in May 1974 in Denver, USA. Those taking part, with ladies and guests, numbered 85. In the anniversary year 70 members of the convent had been traced. Forty-one members took part in the anniversary. All the members of the global presidium were active students admitted outside Latvia. On celebrating its anniversary Philyronia joined the Latvian Freedom Fund.

In the years that followed, as the admission of new members dried up and the number of philisters of the Riga period fell as they passed away, in the New York chapter above all, Philyronia has for the present been unable to send a representative to L!K!A!. The present number of active members is 40. The global presidium publishes an internal journal of news and articles, Philyronias Vēstnesis.

Two members of Philyronia have been awarded the Order of Lāčplēsis, 3rd class, two the Iron Cross, 1st class, and one the Iron Cross, 2nd class. Nine members have earned a place in the Latvju Enciklopēdija through their work.
