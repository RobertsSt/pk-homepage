## About Beveronija

![Members of Beveronija in violet caps and colour sashes with the fraternity’s flag at the foot of a monument](@/assets/fraternities/beveronija/photo-1.jpg)

Founding dates:

- founding convent on 9 March 1922;
- founding commers on 4 May 1922;
- colours first worn in public on 23 October 1923.

Guarantor of the comment – Lettonia\
Active chapters abroad – Chicago, New York, Melbourne\
Active members – 75

## Pastimes

![Seven men in violet sports shirts with footballs on a basketball court](@/assets/fraternities/beveronija/photo-2.jpg)

The members of Beveronija regularly play team sports such as volleyball, basketball and football, turn out in numbers for the zole card tournaments held among the fraternities, and take an active part in service in the National Guard (Zemessardze).

## History

![A historical black-and-white group photograph: several dozen members of the fraternity in caps, those seated in the front row wearing colour sashes](@/assets/fraternities/beveronija/photo-3.jpg)

The founding of the fraternity Beveronija is closely bound up with the unique flowering of Latvian national life that followed Latvia’s War of Independence. The founding of the new Latvian fraternity was furthered by the national enthusiasm and the intellectual strivings that shaped academic life in the first years of the independent Latvian state and of what was then the Latvian Higher School. The founders of Beveronija were 16 matriculated students of the Latvian Higher School from eight different faculties.

Founding the new fraternity took much work and effort, but the founders of Beveronija, supported by the members of the first semesters, overcame every obstacle with great enthusiasm. In the first years of its existence Beveronija admitted ten honorary philisters.
