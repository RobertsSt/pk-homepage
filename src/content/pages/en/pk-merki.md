“In order to gather the young people who study and to bring them up in a spirit of loyalty to state and nation, to prepare capable workers for the state and for society, to develop among students an ethical and aesthetic sense and an individual sense of honour, and to cultivate social life during their studies, the students of the University of Latvia unite in separate fraternities. \[..\] To achieve their aims, the separate fraternities unite in a union, the Presidium Convent.” “To unite the fraternities, which are student societies of the University of Latvia, so as to further the aims of the fraternities by concerted action and to regulate relations among the fraternities.”
