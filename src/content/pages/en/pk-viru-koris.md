The Men’s Choir of the Presidium Convent is a choir in which only members of student fraternities sing. A student fraternity is a student organisation that unites male students in academic education and those of its members who have completed their studies, the philisters. Education, diligence, friendship and a sense of honour underlie a fraternity’s aims. The Presidium Convent is the governing body of the union of Latvia’s student fraternities. All 23 student fraternities active in Latvia have joined the Presidium Convent. Among the men of the fraternities the idea of forming a choir arose soon after the Presidium Convent was founded. On 3 February 1925 a meeting to found the choir was called in the convent quarters of Lettonia, attended by 11 members from almost all the fraternities then in existence. The Presidium Convent not only gave the choir its name, the P!K! Men’s Choir, but also supported it materially. In the 1930s the P!K! Men’s Choir became the country’s representative choir both in Latvia and abroad, and in the eighteen years of its existence it prepared 251 songs for men’s choir by 61 composers, about 80 of them first performances. The choir’s last concert before the long Soviet occupation took place on 13 May 1944 in Jaunpils. In 1993 the choir briefly resumed its activity under the direction of Edgars Račevskis. In 1999 it resumed in earnest under Edgars Linde. In 2005 Arnis Paurs took over as the choir’s artistic director.
